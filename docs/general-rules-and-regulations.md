# Rules & Regulations

## Before You Come to Play

The following rules apply to everyone using the courts and facilities at St. Sebastian's College. Please read them before your first session. By making a booking, you agree to follow these rules.

---

## Parking Is at Your Own Risk

- The College accepts no responsibility for vehicles parked on the premises or for belongings left inside them.
- Vehicles must be parked only in designated parking areas.
- Vehicles must not be parked in restricted areas, including behind the swimming pool.
- Parking restrictions during school activities, examinations, public events, or special functions must be observed.
- Parking within the College premises does not create any entitlement or reserved parking rights.

---

## Don't Wait in the Car Park

- The car park is not a waiting, gathering, or meeting area.
- Please leave the parking area promptly once your session has ended.

---

## No Medical Facilities On Site

- There is no first-aid room or permanent medical staff available on site.
- In an emergency, call **1990 (Suwa Seriya Ambulance Service)**.
- Then contact the Sports Office:
  - **Bookings:** 076 567 4350
  - **Technical:** 077 786 7766

---

## Session Cut Short by Weather or Power Failure

- If extreme weather prevents or interrupts play, contact the Sports Office on the same day.
- Do not rebook the session yourself.
- Refunds or rebooking options resulting from weather interruptions will be assessed by the Sports Office on a case-by-case basis.
- In the event of an unexpected power failure affecting facility operations, the Sports Office will advise available refund or rebooking options where applicable.

---

## Play at Your Own Risk

- You are responsible for your own safety and fitness to participate.
- Users participate entirely at their own risk.
- Users are responsible for their personal belongings at all times.

---

## Children Must Be Supervised

- Players under 16 years of age must be accompanied by a responsible adult who remains on the premises throughout the session.
- Additional supervision requirements may apply to specific facilities such as the swimming pool.

---

## Arriving and Leaving

- Arrive close to your booking start time.
- The court or facility is not available before your booking begins.
- Sessions end on the hour and participants must vacate the facility promptly.
- Use only the courts and facilities included in your booking.
- School buildings, classrooms, offices, and restricted areas are out of bounds.
- Carry a valid NIC, Student ID, Sports ID, or other acceptable identification and present it upon request.
- Vehicle access restrictions may apply during school events, tournaments, or special activities.

---

## Security and Access

- All visitors and vehicles entering the premises may be subject to security checks.
- Users must cooperate with College security personnel at all times.
- Entry may be refused where security requirements are not met.

---

## Facilities and Amenities

- Common College washroom facilities are available for users.
- Dedicated washroom facilities may not be available for individual sports facilities.
- Users must help maintain cleanliness throughout the premises.
- Where waste bins are unavailable, users must dispose of their waste responsibly and take litter away with them.
- Facilities must be left in a clean and usable condition after use.

---

## On the Courts and Facilities

- Every court and facility may have additional sport-specific rules. Please read and follow them before use.
- Wear suitable footwear and clothing appropriate for the sport and playing surface.
- Report any damage, defects, hazards, or unsafe conditions to staff immediately.
- Leave courts and facilities in the same condition in which you found them.
- Floodlights and facility equipment may only be operated by authorised staff.
- Follow all instructions given by College staff, Sports Office personnel, and facility administrators.

---

## Coaches and Training

- Personal coaches, instructors, and trainers may conduct sessions only with prior approval from the College.
- Coaches must comply with all College policies and facility regulations.
- Coaches must not engage in activities that conflict with the interests or policies of St. Sebastian's College.
- Any breach of these requirements may result in cancellation of bookings or withdrawal of facility access.

---

## Damage and Liability

- Users are responsible for any damage caused to courts, equipment, buildings, grounds, furniture, lighting, or any other College property during their booking period.
- Repair or replacement costs may be charged to the individual, group, club, or organisation responsible for the booking.
- All damage must be reported immediately.

---

## Safety and Weather

- Stop play immediately and seek shelter if lightning or unsafe weather conditions occur.
- Report injuries to the Sports Office or available staff as soon as possible.
- Bring drinking water and take adequate breaks during hot weather.
- Rebooking or refund decisions arising from weather-related interruptions are determined by the Sports Office.

---

## Conduct

- No alcohol, smoking, vaping, illegal drugs, toxic substances, or prohibited items are permitted anywhere on College premises.
- No abusive, threatening, offensive, discriminatory, or inappropriate behaviour towards staff, players, students, or visitors.
- Follow all instructions issued by the Sports Office, facility staff, security personnel, and College authorities.
- No pets are permitted on the premises unless specifically authorised.
- St. Sebastian's College is a working educational institution. Visitors must respect the campus, its staff, students, facilities, and neighbouring community.

---

## Your Booking

- Only the person who made the booking and their authorised participants may use the booked session.
- Bookings may not be transferred, resold, or assigned to another party.
- Facilities may be unavailable during public holidays, examinations, school functions, maintenance work, or special events.
- Serious or repeated breaches of these rules may result in cancellation of bookings, removal from the premises, suspension of user accounts, or future booking restrictions.

---

## Refunds and Cancellations

- Refund and cancellation requests are subject to the applicable Sports Office policies.
- Users must contact the Sports Office directly regarding refund requests.
- Refunds or credits due to weather interruptions, facility issues, or power outages will be assessed on a case-by-case basis.
- PayHere Refund Policy – Key Points:
    Instant Refund: If a payment is refunded on the same day, before the end of the day, it is an Instant Refund. It goes back to the customer’s card immediately, in real time, as a payment void.
    Delayed Refund: If a payment is refunded after it has been paid out to the merchant’s bank account (after 2 days), it is a Delayed Refund. It can take 5–10 days, or longer, to show on the customer’s card.
    Day-end processing: Delayed Refunds are processed at the end of the day. The 5–10 days start after PayHere processes the refund.
    No refund fee: Refunds are free. The customer gets back the full amount, including the payment processing fee.
    Full refunds only: PayHere does not support partial refunds. A partial refund must be made manually.
    Card payments can be refunded through PayHere: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover and Diners Club.
    Non-card payments cannot be refunded through PayHere: Frimi, Genie, eZcash, mCash and Vishwa. These must be refunded manually by bank transfer to the customer’s bank account.
    Refund status stages:
    Waiting funds: PayHere is waiting to recover the funds.
    Refund processing: The funds have been recovered and the refund is in progress.
    Refund processed: The refund is done and will reach the card within 5–10 days.
